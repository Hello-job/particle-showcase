# Third-party source and assets

This starter is a local port and extension of the public OpenAI GPT-6 Astra particle experience. Original chunk/module provenance and retained rendering parameters are recorded in `src/astra/README.md`. The original site is https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/.

The bundled OpenAI Sans fonts, OpenAI wordmark and Astra fallback poster originate from that page. DeepSeek paths originate from https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V2/blob/main/figures/logo.svg. The custom titlecase DeepSeek wordmark combines translated official lowercase contours with uppercase D/S outlines from the bundled font; it is not the official logotype.

Kimi paths originate from the official Moonshot AI Branding Guide at https://moonshotai.github.io/Branding-Guide/. The Z.ai emblem originates from https://github.com/zai-org/GLM-4.5/blob/main/resources/logo.svg, with its outer frame removed. GLM lettering is outlined from the bundled font, not traced from an official GLM wordmark.

Original source links are retained in the shape modules. npm dependencies retain their respective package licenses. This starter does not grant a new license for the extracted renderer, third-party fonts or brand assets and must not be represented as wholly original or uniformly MIT licensed.
